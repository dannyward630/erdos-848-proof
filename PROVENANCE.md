# Provenance and review boundary

Danny Ward directed this project, selected the evidence and publication scope,
and is the public maintainer. Research, checker implementation, proof drafting,
source comparison, and adversarial review were carried out in a multi-agent
OpenAI Codex workflow under that direction.

The workflow deliberately separated proof construction from review. When this
repository says that a checker or review was “independent,” it means a distinct
implementation or separately assigned internal review lane did not certify its
own work. It does not mean independent external reproduction, anonymous peer
review, or acceptance by the Erdős Problems project.

The durable evidence is the repository itself: exact source identities,
certificate formats, computations, negative controls, proof dependencies,
failed lemmas, and reproduction commands. Chat transcripts are not proof
dependencies. Reviewer feedback was incorporated only when its mathematical
claim or exact counterexample could be checked and recorded in these files.

As of 11 August 2026, this is an unrefereed public proof candidate. The
mathematical all-$N$ argument is recorded as proved in the project ledger, but
the repository's stricter completion node remains open pending a fresh root
six-stage computation and an independent clean ART-006 Lean build, trust-zero
replay, axiom report, and dependency audit on the documented host.
